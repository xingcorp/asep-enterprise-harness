# Public Company Patterns — Interpretation, not imitation

Public materials consistently show that effective enterprise use is a systems problem:

- OpenAI emphasizes agent-friendly repositories, externalized state, tests and harness engineering.
- GitHub evaluates a shared harness across models using resolution, tokens, cost, variance and online experiments, while retaining human merge accountability.
- Google/Siemens public material describes knowledge fabrics/graphs for large brownfield modernization rather than placing the entire codebase into a context window.
- DORA frames AI as an amplifier of existing engineering systems; delivery stability and feedback quality remain essential.

These are public/vendor accounts, not complete disclosures of internal systems. ASEP adopts the recurring architectural patterns—durable state, bounded tasks, evidence, platform governance, knowledge indexing and human risk ownership—without assuming vendor-reported performance transfers to another organization.
