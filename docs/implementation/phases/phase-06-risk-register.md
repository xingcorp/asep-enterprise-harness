# Phase 6 — Risk Register (DRAFT v1)

> Delivery, design and operating risks for brownfield and production hardening. Base `f613c7f`. Severity = impact ×
> likelihood (HIGH, MED, LOW). "Owner action" says what happens when the trigger fires; nothing here is an accepted
> risk until the user or the controller accepts it in `phase-06-review-log.md`.
